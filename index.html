Ripple

Only the stories that change lives inside a country, or send effects across its borders. Picked by an AI agent that reads world news around the clock.

What gets published

An agent reads world news and official feeds every two hours. It groups reports about the same event, then rates each story on how many people it affects, how severe it is, whether it reaches other countries, and whether it will still matter next month. Only high scorers appear here.

Reading a story

The signal bars show impact from 1 to 5. Each story splits into what changes inside the country and what spills across borders. “Early report” means few outlets have confirmed it so far.

Read this carefully

  • Summaries are written by an AI from headlines and short feed text, so check the linked sources for details.
  • Photos belong to the outlets that published them. They are shown as small previews with credit and link to the original report.
